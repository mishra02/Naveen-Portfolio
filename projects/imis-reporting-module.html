Integrated Management Information System (IMIS) — Reporting Module

Separate web-based reporting application for water sources, government schemes, and current water-source status.

Project overview

The reporting module presents information about water sources and the government schemes applied to those sources. Reports are displayed in tabular form and can be downloaded or printed in multiple formats.

Public project URL: https://ejalshakti.gov.in/imisreports/IMISReportLogin.aspx
